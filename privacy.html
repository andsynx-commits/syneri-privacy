Syneri privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Syneri is a private assistant used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What Syneri accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: to summarise the owner's mail and notice bills and subscriptions.
  • Gmail drafts: to save reply drafts in the owner's own Gmail. Syneri never sends, deletes, labels or changes email; the owner reviews each draft and sends it himself.

How the data is used and kept

  • Data is used only to help the owner, at the owner's request.
  • The Gmail sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Email content is processed to answer the owner and is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Syneri's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to disconnect

Remove Syneri's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
